# Product Overview

## MedCore BD — Medical Equipment E-Commerce Platform

MedCore BD is a comprehensive B2B and B2C e-commerce platform for medical equipment, surgical instruments, laboratory reagents, and hospital supplies in Bangladesh.

### Core Business Model

- **Primary Market**: Bangladesh healthcare sector (hospitals, clinics, diagnostic centers, laboratories)
- **Customer Segments**: 
  - B2B customers (hospitals, clinics, diagnostic centers) with bulk discounts (8-30%) and credit terms (30-90 days)
  - B2C retail customers (individual healthcare professionals, small clinics)
- **Product Categories**: Diagnostic Equipment, Surgical Instruments, Laboratory Reagents, Hospital Machines, Lab Equipment, PPE & Safety, Dental Equipment, Implants & Ortho
- **Key Differentiators**: DGDA registered products, ISO 13485 certified, free installation in Dhaka, cold chain delivery for reagents

### Target Keywords & SEO Focus

The platform is optimized for Bangladesh-specific medical equipment searches:
- Primary: "medical equipment Bangladesh", "diagnostic equipment Dhaka", "reagent supplier BD"
- Long-tail: "ECG machine price Bangladesh", "HbA1c kit price BD", "surgical instruments supplier Dhaka"
- Locale: en_BD (English - Bangladesh)

### Key Features

- **Product Catalog**: 10,000+ medical products from global brands (Siemens, GE, Roche, Abbott, Mindray, etc.)
- **B2B Portal**: Dedicated dashboard with bulk pricing, credit terms, account manager
- **Reagent Store**: Specialized section for laboratory reagents with cold chain management
- **Order Management**: Quote requests, order tracking, returns processing
- **Payment Integration**: bKash, Nagad, credit card, bank transfer
- **Analytics**: Google Analytics 4, performance monitoring, sales analytics
- **Compliance**: DGDA registration tracking, CE certification, ISO 13485 standards
