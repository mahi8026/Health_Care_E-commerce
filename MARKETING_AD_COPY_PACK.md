| Campaign goal | Use section |

| Your brand to new people (prospecting) | §1 |

| Push a flash sale / coupon | §5 |
| Google Search campaigns | §6 |

**Creative rule of thumb (Meta):**
- Feed ads: 4:5 vertical or 1:1 square image (real product photos from your
  site, not stock).
- Stories/Reels: 9:16 video 5–15s (product + price pop + "Order on WhatsApp").
- Hook in the first 2 seconds: show the product filling the frame.

**Important compliance note for BD:** avoid disease-treatment promises that
could be read as medical claims. Say "DGDA-registered," "warranty-backed,"
"hospital-grade," "for professionals." Don't say "cures X" or "treats Y."
