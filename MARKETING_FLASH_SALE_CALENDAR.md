# 📅 MediportBD — Flash-Sale Calendar (Bangladesh Health Events & Seasonal Moments)

A 12-month calendar of **high-intent sales moments** for medical equipment,
lab reagents, and surgical supplies in Bangladesh. Each entry includes:
- **What** to promote
- **Who** to target
- **Suggested discount / offer**
- **Ad copy hook** (English + বাংলা)
- **Lead time** (when to start preparing)

---

## How to use this calendar

1. **Pick 2–3 moments per month** — don't try to run them all.
2. **Start preparing 7–10 days before** the event (creative, coupon code, ad setup).
3. **Launch the sale 2–3 days before** the peak day (buyers plan ahead for health events).
4. **Use the matching ad copy** from `MARKETING_AD_COPY_PACK.md` and WhatsApp scripts from `MARKETING_WHATSAPP_BROADCASTS.md`.
5. **Track results** in Admin → Marketing → Dashboard.

**Coupon naming convention:** `{EVENT}{YEAR}` → `BISHWA25`, `EID25`, `NOV25`

## 💡 Pro tips for Bangladesh market

1. **Bilingual everything** — always run English + বাংলা versions of ads; বাংলা often wins for B2C.
2. **bKash/Nagad/COD prominently** — mention payment methods in every offer; trust signal.
3. **Free delivery threshold** — ৳30,000–50,000 is the sweet spot for BD medical equipment.
4. **B2B credit terms** — always mention "30–90 day credit" for hospital/clinic ads.
5. **DGDA registration** — include "DGDA-registered" in every ad; it's a trust differentiator.
6. **WhatsApp CTA** — always include "Message on WhatsApp" as primary CTA; BD buyers prefer chat.
7. **Friday/Saturday low** — reduce ad spend on Fri/Sat; peak engagement Sun–Thu.
8. **Load-shedding season** (Apr–Jun) — push voltage stabilizers, inverters for equipment.
9. **Flood season** (Jun–Sep) — push water testing kits, emergency medical supplies to affected areas.
10. **Government budget cycle** (Jun–Jul) — hospitals get new budgets; push B2B hard in July.
