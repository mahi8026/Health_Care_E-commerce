## 0. Golden rules (read first)

1. **Only message people who opted in** — existing B2B clients, prior
   inquirers, website WhatsApp clicks are fine to re-contact; don't cold-blast
   random numbers (BD telecom rules + Meta policy).
2. **Time windows (BD local):** B2B → Sun–Thu, 10:00–13:00 or 15:00–17:00.
   B2C → 11:00–20:00. Never before 10 AM or after 9 PM.
3. **Keep it short.** Aim ≤ 8 lines. WhatsApp previews cut around 2–3 lines —
   hook first.
4. **One offer per message.** No walls of text.
5. **Always include a next action:** "Reply OK", "Send us your list", a link,
   or a clear question. End with a question to force a reply.
6. **Never "HI"/"HELLO" alone** — always add value or a reason to respond.
7. **Frequency:** max 2–4 messages/month per contact. Mix value (tips, new
   arrivals) with offers.
8. **Suffix when relevant:** "To stop updates, reply STOP" (esp. for bulk
   lists — good practice + policy-friendly).

different segments; compare open/reply rates in WhatsApp Business stats and
keep the winner.

month, and always honor "STOP" opt-outs immediately.
